# Formula1-AI: Time-Series & Simulation Engine

## 1. Time-Series Forecasting
F1 data is inherently sequential. The project treats lap times, track evolution, and weather as time-series problems.

* **Validation Strategy:** Walk-forward validation (rolling window).
* **Baselines:** ARIMA and Prophet are used to establish baseline trend detection for lap time evolution and tire degradation over a stint.
* **Decomposition:** Seasonal decomposition isolates track grip evolution (trend) from fuel burn (seasonality/noise).

## 2. Monte Carlo Simulation Engine
The `src/simulation/` module runs thousands of probabilistic scenarios to predict complex, multi-variable outcomes.

### Race Simulator
* Simulates a full race lap-by-lap.
* Takes grid position, predicted tire degradation, historical pit stop timing, and safety car probability as inputs.
* Outputs a probability matrix of finishing positions for every driver.

### Season Simulator
* Simulates the remaining races in a championship.
* Accounts for expected constructor upgrades (momentum/trend) and historical track compatibility.
* Outputs probability distributions for the Drivers' and Constructors' Championship winners.
